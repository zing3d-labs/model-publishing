## Changelog

**2026-08-14** — Fixed snaps coming out as separate objects in the slicer. Each snap's nubs sat exactly flush against the snap body, sharing a surface rather than overlapping it, and the bottom click holes left too thin a bridge in the Lite core — so the snaps could slice as loose, disconnected pieces instead of one solid part. Every panel now slices as a single connected body. Outside dimensions are unchanged, so this is a drop-in replacement for anything you have already printed.
