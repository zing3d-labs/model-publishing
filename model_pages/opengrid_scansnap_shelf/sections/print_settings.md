## Print Settings

0.2mm layer height, 2 walls, 15% infill, no supports — the settings the project file carries, in PLA on a P1S.

**Print the halves on their side, the way the plates are already laid out.** This is the one thing not to change. A shelf fails at its layer lines, and standing each half up on its side puts those lines across the load rather than along it — that is what "sturdy shelf" refers to in the original design, and it is worth more than any infill setting. Laying a half flat on the bed would print faster and give you a shelf that snaps at the root.

Both halves sit at an angle on their plates rather than square to the bed. That is deliberate — at 280mm the depth does not fit a 256mm bed straight on. Leave the placement alone and both halves fit; straighten them up and they will not.

Both halves and the snap plate print without supports as laid out.

The surface texture you can see on the shelf is deliberate — it hides layer lines and print artefacts. It looks coarse on screen and feels smooth in the hand.
