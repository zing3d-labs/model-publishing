## What's in the Download

One 3MF with three plates:

1. **Left** — one shelf half
2. **Right** — the other half
3. **Snaps** — 8 openConnect snaps and 8 screws, enough to mount the shelf with 4 per side

The two halves are not the same part mirrored, so print one of each rather than the same plate twice.

There are no STLs and no size variants. If you want a different shelf — other dimensions, a different truss, no texture — go to mitufy's generator, linked at the bottom, and make exactly the one you want. This listing is one configuration of it, saved because it fits a scanner.
