The shelf is a deep, square deck with a trussed underside. A back panel sits flat against the grid, the deck runs 280mm out from the wall, and a tapering truss below it carries the load back into the mount rather than hanging it off the deck's root. It is one shelf, printed as two halves that join down the middle.

The split is not about bed width. Each half prints standing on its side, so the shelf's width becomes height on the bed — and an unsplit 280mm-wide shelf would need 280mm of vertical clearance, more than most printers have. Halved, each piece stands 140mm.

Nothing about it is glued or bracketed. Each half drops onto openConnect snaps already fitted to your grid, and locking snaps hold it down.
