## Assembly

1. Print both halves and the snap plate.
2. Fit 4 openConnect snaps to your grid for each half — 8 in total — using the screws.
3. Drop the left half onto its snaps, then the right, bringing their inner ends together. The joining ends are the ones with no side edge; the finished side edges belong at the two far ends.
4. Check the deck reads flat across the join before you put anything on it.

The halves meet edge to edge and are held in line by the grid itself, so there is nothing to glue and no hardware between them.
