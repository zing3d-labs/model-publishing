The pocket is a wedge. A tall back panel sits flat against the wall, a shorter front panel rakes outward so sheets lean back and stay readable, and tapering side panels close it in. A notch in the front edge lets you grab a whole stack at once instead of picking at the top sheet.

It hangs on an openGrid wall through openConnect slots cut straight into the back panel — there is no bracket and nothing to glue. The part drops down onto openConnect snaps already fitted to your grid, so gravity does the holding, and locking nubs in the corner slots keep it from lifting off by accident.
