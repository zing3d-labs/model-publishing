## Print Settings

**No supports required.** Print it standing the way it hangs — pocket floor on the bed, back panel upright — which is how the plates are already laid out. The front panel leans outward only 12° from vertical, so it holds itself up, and no perforation cell spans more than 12mm.

Standard settings work everywhere except the wall. The back panel is what carries a loaded inbox on the grid, so give it three walls rather than two; 15% infill is plenty for the rest.

Check your build volume before you start. The US Letter and A4 plates are 224mm wide and stand **224mm tall**, so they want a 256mm bed and roughly 230mm of Z. The A5 plates are 168 × 81mm and 168mm tall, which fits almost anything.

If you customize the perforation, pick a `Perforation_Wall_Thickness` that is a whole number of extrusion widths. The 2mm default is five passes of a 0.4mm nozzle, which comes out as solid wall instead of a line and a sliver of gap fill.
