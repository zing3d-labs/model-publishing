## Mounting

No assembly — the inbox is a single-piece print.

To hang it, fit openConnect snaps into the grid positions you want to use, then lower the inbox onto them. The slots are cut so the part slides down over the snaps, which means the load is carried by the grid rather than by the connectors' grip, and the locking nubs in the corner slots stop it lifting back off on its own.

Take the weight into account when you choose how many positions to use. A loaded inbox is heavy, and the back panel offers a full 8 × 8 grid of slots precisely so you can spread that load over as many connectors as you like.
