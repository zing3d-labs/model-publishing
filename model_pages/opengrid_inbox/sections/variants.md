## What's in the Download

One print profile carrying nine plates — three paper sizes crossed with the three panel patterns. They are alternatives, not a set: pick the plate you want and print that one.

**Hex honeycomb** — the default, and the large tray in the photos

- US Letter — 224 × 89 × 224mm
- A4 — 224 × 91 × 224mm
- A5 — 168 × 81 × 168mm

**Square lattice** — same shape and dimensions, straight-edged pattern

- US Letter, A4, A5

**Solid** — no perforation anywhere, about a third more material and a longer print

- US Letter, A4, A5

Anything else — a different sheet size, a deeper pocket, a shallower rake, a scalloped grab cutout instead of the notch — comes out of the customizer. The small envelope tray in the photos is this same model with a custom sheet size, solid panels and the scallop cutout, and nothing else changed.
